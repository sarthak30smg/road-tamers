# What's New

## Road Tamers 1.9.4 — October 20, 2026

- **Name your racer on first launch.** The name is saved on the device and appears in the game and leaderboard. Existing saves without a name are prompted when continued.
- **Motor Hall showroom.** Walk around a three-quarter-view showroom, inspect six featured road cars, then meet Max to browse and buy from the full collection.
- **Smoother showroom movement.** Directional movement now eases between positions, has a walk cycle, and respects display-car collisions.
- **Expanded CarDex.** Browse 75 cars with model-specific artwork, prices, stats, and top speeds; engine tuning raises top speed.
- **More ways to play.** Photo mode, racer customization, solo Time Trial and Traffic Escape challenges, and eight story championships.
